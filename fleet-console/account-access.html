Lost driver credential?

Contact your fleet administrator. They can issue a replacement and revoke the old credential in the fleet cockpit. Never post a credential in chat or a service report.

Password reset, registration and verification

These workflows belong to the configured identity provider. If no link is configured below, your administrator must provide the provider's account-access address. This application cannot claim to send a reset or verification email.

Sign out

Use Disconnect in the portal to clear its active tab credential. Signed identity-provider tokens also expire according to the provider's policy. Administrator-provisioned driver credentials can be revoked by fleet staff.
