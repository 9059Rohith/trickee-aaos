Send an advisory to the assigned driver's browser inbox. It appears when the driver refreshes or keeps the companion open. This does not control the vehicle or send a push notification.

Send a manual pickup and destination proposal to the assigned driver. Acceptance records the driver's decision; it does not create a partner order, start a trip, or control the vehicle. Confirm addresses and availability before sending.
