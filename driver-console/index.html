    08 / PICKUP PROPOSALS

    Review a pickup

    These are manual proposals from your fleet. Check the pickup, destination and message before accepting. Acceptance records your response; it does not start a trip or confirm a partner order. Expired and withdrawn proposals cannot be accepted.

    Connect to review pickup proposals.

    09 / YOUR PHONE DATA

    Export or erase trip GPS and motion

    Export downloads the cloud samples associated with this driver. Erasure requires every trip to be complete. It withdraws trip phone-data consent, erases cloud samples and batch receipts, and clears queued points for this driver's listed trips in this browser. Other devices and existing backups require separate cleanup.
